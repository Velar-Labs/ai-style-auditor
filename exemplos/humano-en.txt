I spent twenty minutes yesterday looking for my phone charger. It was in my backpack, in the usual pocket. My son laughed and said I do this every week. He's probably right.

I bought a spare charger at the corner store for nine dollars. One stays in the living room, the other in the car. On Tuesday, the battery still died in the middle of a client call. I used the front desk phone and sorted it out in five minutes. Oh well.

The real issue was never the charger. I just don't check before leaving. My wife has given up reminding me. So I taped a note to the door: "charger?". It's working so far.
